Quick Reference

This page is a quick reference only. Approved residents should use the Resident Portal document library for full governing documents and forms.

Architectural Control

  • No structure, fence, barrier, exterior appearance change, paint, re-roofing, addition, attachment, deletion, or landscaping change should begin without written consent of the Architectural Control Committee.
  • No exterior television or radio antennas should be installed without written authorization.
  • Violations may result in notification and follow up by the property management company.
  • Use the Resident Portal for architectural change requests and related document access.

Restrictive Covenants

  • Mobile equipment, garbage cans, wood piles, and storage piles should be kept screened from street and neighbor view.
  • Recreational or commercial vehicles, boats, trailers, camping trailers, motorcycles, and similar items should be kept in a garage or screened from view.
  • Grass, weeds, and vegetation should be kept mowed and cleared. Dead trees, shrubs, vines, and plants should be removed promptly.
  • Full covenant documents are available in the Resident Portal document library.
