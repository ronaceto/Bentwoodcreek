Reminders and Alerts

Seasonal reminders and practical neighborhood notices for Bentwood Creek homeowners.

Fall and Winter Cleanup

  • Please do not blow or rake leaves into neighborhood streets.
  • Homeowners are responsible for leaves in their own yard and curb areas.
  • Wait until Sunday evening to place yard bags or bulky items out for Monday pickup.

Mail Delivery Issues

Mail delivery is not an HOA responsibility, but similar street names and duplicate house numbers in the area can cause confusion. If you receive mail intended for a neighbor, please deliver it to the correct address when possible and make the Post Office aware of the issue.

Collierville Post Office: 901.853.0994

Garbage and Recycle Carts

Covenants require garbage and recycle carts to be stored so they are not visible from the street or neighboring properties.
