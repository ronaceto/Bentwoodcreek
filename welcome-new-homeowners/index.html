New Homeowners

Welcome to Bentwood Creek

On behalf of the Bentwood Creek Homeowners Association, we would like to extend a warm welcome to you and your family. The fact that you have chosen Bentwood Creek as your new home is a major complement to our neighborhood.

We are sure that you chose Bentwood Creek in part for its natural beauty as well as the pride of ownership shown by our homeowners.

The association is managed by an elected board in partnership with Crye-Leike Community Association Management Company. Please familiarize yourself with the Declaration of Covenants and Architectural Controls. Exterior changes, including additions, fencing, paint, roofing, and significant landscaping, require approval before work begins.
