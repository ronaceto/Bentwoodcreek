Safety

Safety and Security

Neighborhood reminders for driving, property awareness, and suspicious activity reporting.

Drive Cautiously

The speed limit in Bentwood Creek is 20 MPH. Please drive carefully and stay aware of walkers, runners, children, and families using the neighborhood streets.

Property Awareness

Please be cautious about leaving garage doors open and avoid leaving valuables or personal property visible in vehicles.

Report Suspicious Activity

If you notice suspicious activity, report it to the police and notify the association so residents can be alerted when appropriate.
