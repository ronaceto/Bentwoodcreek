Our Association

Bentwood Creek HOA

The association is managed by an elected board of directors in partnership with Crye-Leike Community Association Management Company.

Association Structure

Bentwood Creek homeowners are represented by volunteer board members and committee chairs who help maintain neighborhood standards, communication, and community priorities.

  • President
  • Vice-President
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Architectural Control Committee Chair

Resident involvement is always welcome. Committees and volunteer efforts help keep the neighborhood organized, responsive, and connected.

Management Partner

Crye-Leike Community Association Management Company supports the HOA with covenant control, financial and accounting services, collections, distribution services, and owner support.
